# Research

Read [connection and approvals](../../references/runtime.md) first.

Read the audience, existing voice guide and accepted marketing question. Choose sources relevant to that question: public discussions, customer and competitor reviews, or authorized support and interview material.

Use available host search, authorized browsing, connected reviews or supplied exports. Record source links, collection dates and coverage. Respect access restrictions; do not extract browser sessions or install a research helper.

Keep first-hand situations separate from advice and speculation. Distinguish literal quotes from paraphrases and new hooks. Count recurring language within the observed sample; engagement alone is not proof of demand. Apply quotation limits and remove personal or confidential details from derived copy.

Organize evidence by supported audience, trigger, objection, alternative and desired outcome. Create new hooks grounded in that evidence, with source pointers and clearly labeled rewrites. Do not turn a customer's statement into a fabricated testimonial.

Preserve an existing voice guide. Propose changes from observed language and explicit preferences, then save accepted changes through supported documents or return them in chat. Research never authorizes posting, replies or outreach.
