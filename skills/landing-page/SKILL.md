# Landing Page

Read [connection and approvals](../../references/runtime.md) first.

Read the page and the destination of its call to action, then the existing voice, customer research and accepted marketing plan. Identify who arrives, what they already know and the action the page must earn.

Use public competitors and authorized research to identify specific alternatives and objections. Distinguish what a customer actually said from copy inspired by it. Choose a frame based on the product's concrete advantage and available proof.

Draft the page as a connected argument: a clear promise, the demonstration or evidence needed to believe it, answers to relevant objections and a useful next action. Never invent testimonials, customer logos, ratings or performance numbers. Flag missing proof plainly.

Deliver exact replacement copy, preserving the user's requested layout or scope. If implementation is requested and project editing is available, use that authorized workflow; otherwise provide a reviewable handoff. Do not imply copy edits have been deployed.

Read `get_documentation` topics `tracking` and `marketing-review` when evaluating conversion. Compare the intended action with consistent audience, period and denominator; traffic mix or simultaneous changes can confound a result.
