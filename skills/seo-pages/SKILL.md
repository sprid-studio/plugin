# Seo Pages

Read [connection and approvals](../../references/runtime.md) first.

Read the existing website, customer language and accepted marketing plan. Read `get_documentation` topics `queries`, `metrics` and `marketing-review` for connected search evidence.

Identify relevant queries, search intent and the destination that can satisfy it. Inspect existing pages before proposing another. Use available Search Console or Bing evidence with dates, country and device stated. Missing access is not evidence of no demand.

Draft pages that answer the actual question with product-specific demonstrations or evidence. Keep structured data consistent with visible facts. Avoid near-duplicate pages and invented location or review claims. Check canonical, indexing directives, internal links and rendered content when authorized inspection is available.

For AI discovery, record exact prompts, engine, date and repeated observations. Separate a citation, a product recommendation and attributable customer activity. A single answer is not a stable ranking.

Deliver the page or exact implementation brief and the measurement plan. Publishing requires the user's authorization. Evaluate qualified visits and product outcomes as well as impressions; explain when coverage or attribution cannot support the conclusion.
