# Reels

Read [connection and approvals](../../references/runtime.md) first.

Read `get_documentation` for `chat` and `reel-first-seconds`. Read the account's voice, audience and templates; choose the format and duration from the demonstration, story or useful example.

Make the opening frame understandable and give viewers a reason to continue. Separate reusable media from localized text where possible. Fictional people can explain or react; never invent personal testimony. Use media and music with appropriate rights.

Check the connection's video capabilities before promising production. Use exposed video operations and their revision fields. Choose which destinations receive the video explicitly. Custom app recordings and unsupported animation require supplied assets or a production handoff; this package does not install a renderer.

Before a metered render, show the returned quote or allowance and obtain the required approval. Inspect the actual output for text, crops, timing, transitions and audio. Show the preview, then follow the post workflow for human approval and publication receipts.

Compare similar published videos with duration, sample and time window visible. Average watch time does not measure retention at a particular second. Report missing metrics and judge acquisition against visits and product outcomes where measurement supports them.
