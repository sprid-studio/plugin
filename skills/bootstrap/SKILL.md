# Bootstrap

Read [connection and approvals](../../references/runtime.md) first.

Read the product's website or store listing, existing positioning and accepted marketing plan. Reuse existing app and content-account records; confirm identity before saving a new profile. A repository and social account are optional.

Read `get_documentation` with topic `marketing-review`. Establish who buys, the concrete benefit, the acquisition destination and what evidence distinguishes a traffic problem from a conversion or retention problem. Missing access is unknown, never proof of missing tracking.

Inspect relevant listing copy, screenshots and the destination's first screen. Quote observed evidence. Preserve the customer's voice and existing work. Recommend the route supported by that evidence and prepare the requested artifact or one that advances that route: copy, a post draft, a measurement plan or a search-page brief.

Save accepted work through available marketing-plan tools. Proposals stay proposals until accepted. Report the artifact, evidence behind the route and the one missing input that could change it.
