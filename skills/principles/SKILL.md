# Principles

Read [connection and approvals](../../references/runtime.md) first.

Read the accepted marketing plan and available customer evidence. Use `get_documentation` topic `marketing-review` for the investigation method and `metrics` for definitions.

Choose an outcome the product can observe: purchases, qualified leads, activation or retained use. Understand who needs it, how they discover it and why they would choose it. Do not prescribe a channel or posting cadence before identifying that path.

Separate reach from demand and attributed revenue from incremental revenue. Test an apparent pattern against sample size, coverage changes and alternative explanations. Compare actual cohorts and contribution economics where data exists; do not invent a lifetime value from a short observation window.

Recommend the route with supporting evidence, the observation that would disprove it and the next artifact or experiment. Keep a proposed strategy distinct from permission to change campaigns or publish content. Preserve explicit user goals and constraints.
