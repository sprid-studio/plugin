# Sprid for Claude.

Plan marketing, draft posts and store copy, study customer language, and review
results with Sprid's hosted connection and the evidence you provide.

Sign in to Sprid when Claude connects. The skills reuse your app, content accounts,
voice and marketing plan. Credentials are entered in secure setup forms.

## What you can do.

The skills cover product diagnosis, service connection, marketing review,
measurement, growth strategy, social posts, reels, screenshots, store copy,
landing pages, search discovery, audience research, competitor research and ads.
They check the tools available to your connection before promising an operation.

Public research and copy drafts can proceed with available browsing or supplied
material. Hosted production uses the capabilities and allowance shown by Sprid.
Store submission and unsupported production steps receive an explicit handoff.
Publishing, replies and paid actions retain their required human approvals.

This distribution contains instructions and a hosted connection. It includes no
local executables and does not read credentials from your computer. Updates arrive
through the plugin directory. For the separately distributed coding-agent package,
see [Sprid for agents](https://sprid.studio/agents).
